Lastmile Gig is a subsidiary of the Affiliated Architecture Group (AAG) — South Africa's premier multi-disciplinary built-environment practice. Built from KwaZulu-Natal, operating nationally.

We are the AI Singularity Operating System for last-mile delivery — a full-stack, blockchain-anchored, IoT-integrated platform serving gig drivers, corporate fleet clients, restaurants, hotels, retail chains, and enterprise logistics operators simultaneously.

Our 21-module ecosystem spans driver rental, contracted fleets, branded storefronts, enterprise logistics, ESG tracking, and agentic AI intelligence — all operating on a polyglot microservices architecture deployed on AWS with global top-1% observability.
